# LoreVia — Business & Legal Plan

---

## Phase 1 — Validate (Now → $1k MRR)

**Entity:** None. Personal account.
**Payments:** Dodo Payments or Paddle connected to your savings account.
**Focus:** 100 free users → prove retention → first 10 paying users.
**Cost:** ~$12 for domain. Nothing else.

---

## Phase 2 — Legitimise ($1k → $3k MRR)

**Entity:** Still no foreign company yet. Too early, compliance cost kills margin.
**Do:** Open a current account at ICICI/Kotak under your own name as a freelancer/sole proprietor. Switch Paddle payouts there. Keep a CA on retainer (~₹15k/year) just for ITR filing.
**Why not incorporate yet:** $2,000+/year in US/Singapore compliance when you're making $1k MRR is 15%+ of revenue gone on paperwork.

---

## Phase 3 — Incorporate Abroad ($3k MRR or first investor conversation)

**Entity:** Delaware C-Corp (US) if targeting US users or US investors. Singapore Pte Ltd if targeting Southeast Asia or staying bootstrapped.

**Delaware is probably right for LoreVia** — users are global, reading apps skew English-speaking markets, US VCs are the biggest pool.

**How:**
- Use **Stripe Atlas** ($500 one-time) — sets up Delaware C-Corp, EIN, and Mercury bank account in one flow entirely online
- Switch from Paddle to Stripe once you have the US entity
- Mercury bank account — best US bank for foreign founders, fully online, no branch visit needed

**Annual compliance cost:**
- Registered agent: ~$50/year
- Delaware franchise tax: $400/year minimum
- Accountant: ~$500–1,500/year at this revenue
- Total: ~$1,000–2,000/year — affordable at $3k MRR

**India side:** As an Indian resident owning a foreign company, RBI/FEMA compliance is required. Tell your CA — they handle it. One-time ~₹30,000 setup. Not optional.

---

## Phase 4 — Scale ($10k MRR+)

- Trademark LoreVia in the US (~$250 per class)
- Get a proper startup lawyer for any investor conversations
- ESOP pool if you hire
- Everything else follows naturally

## Rules

1. **Buy the domain today.** `lorevia.app` — ~$12/year on Namecheap or Porkbun.
2. **No company until $3k MRR or an investor asks.** Registration before PMF is a distraction.
3. **Track every rupee from day one.** Date, amount, source. Spreadsheet. 2 minutes a month.
4. **Never mix personal and business expenses** once you open the current account.
5. **RBI/FEMA compliance is not optional** when you incorporate abroad. Tell your CA immediately.
