## Deliberate boundaries

- Scene lists summarize narrative change; the existing story-bible guide manages reusable canon.
- The finished-draft timeline guide reconstructs chronology; the existing continuity guide audits contradictions across all fact types.
- Knowledge mapping follows information transfer; character tracking covers identities, states, and relationships more broadly.
- Setup and payoff follows reader expectation; subplot tracking follows the life cycle of secondary dramatic action.
- POV auditing concerns narrative access; ensemble balancing concerns contribution and dramatic function.
- Developmental-edit preparation concerns readiness and handoff; feedback planning concerns interpreting notes after they arrive.
- The genre guides apply the shared continuity method to different governing constraints rather than repeating generic plot advice.
